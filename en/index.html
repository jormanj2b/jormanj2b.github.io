Process

From the first message to a live site, usually 5 to 14 days, depending on the project.

  1. First messageTell me what you sell, who it’s for, what you already have (logo, photos, copy), and when you need it. I’ll reply on the same channel.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know you’re not a bot or a scam?

You can check my Freelancer or Workana profile, open every project on this page, and write to me through the form. If that feels safer, hire me on one of those platforms so the payment is protected there. You also pay 50% up front and the rest only when you approve the site.

Why not use Wix or a template?

You can. The difference is that the site ends up in your name, you don’t pay a monthly subscription to keep it online, and it loads faster because it doesn’t ship code you don’t use.

Do I need photos and copy already?

It helps a lot. If you don’t have them, I’ll tell you what photos to take with your phone, and I’ll write the copy with you from what you tell me by message.
